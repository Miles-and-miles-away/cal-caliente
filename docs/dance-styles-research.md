# Dance Styles Research

## Major Social Latin/Partner Dance Styles for the App

Based on research across multiple dance community sources, here are the dance styles most commonly found at social dance events, especially in Japan's Latin dance scene:

### Tier 1 — Core (Most Common at Social Events)
1. **Salsa** — The most popular Latin social dance worldwide (On1/LA, On2/NY, Cuban)
2. **Bachata** — Second most popular, romantic partner dance
3. **Zouk** (Brazilian Zouk / Lambazouk) — Very popular in Japan's dance scene
4. **Kizomba** — African-origin partner dance, growing rapidly worldwide
5. **Merengue** — Dominican Republic origin, often played between salsa sets

### Tier 2 — Common at Events
6. **Cha Cha Cha** — Classic Latin rhythm, both social and competitive
7. **Reggaeton** — Modern Latin urban dance, common at parties
8. **Mambo** — Predecessor to salsa, still danced socially
9. **Cumbia** — Colombian origin, popular across Latin America
10. **Samba** — Brazilian dance, both social and carnival style

### Tier 3 — Specialty / Less Common but Notable
11. **Argentine Tango** — Distinct from ballroom tango, social milongas
12. **Forró** — Brazilian partner dance, growing international scene
13. **Lambada** — Brazilian, evolved into Zouk
14. **Rumba** — Both social and competitive Latin dance
15. **Bolero** — Slow romantic Latin dance
16. **Semba** — Angolan dance, predecessor to Kizomba

### Selected for App (14 styles)
salsa, bachata, zouk, kizomba, merengue, cha-cha-cha, reggaeton, mambo, cumbia, samba, argentine-tango, forro, rumba, semba
